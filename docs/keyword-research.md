## Topic clusters
- **Pillar: Private label clothing** -> custom manufacturing, fabrics, printing & embroidery, tech pack guide, MOQ guide.
- **Pillar: Clothing manufacturer for Europe** -> export services, global reach, shipping/duty guide, EU labelling guide.
- **Pillar: Apparel manufacturer in Pakistan** -> factory, quality, sustainability, about.
Every cluster page links up to its pillar and across to siblings (see RelatedLinks).

## E-E-A-T actions
- Show real people: add a team / founder section with names and photos (needs your input).
- Publish the exact factory address, registration and certificates you hold (do not claim ones you lack).
- Add real, verifiable client reviews and case studies.
- Put an author name and date on every blog post; refresh key pages every 6 months and bump the sitemap date.
- Avoid keyword stuffing and thin or auto-generated text: every post needs original detail from your factory.
